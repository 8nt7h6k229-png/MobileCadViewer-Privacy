MobileCadViewer 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 27 日

產品與用途

MobileCadViewer 是用於離線檢視與審閱本機 DWG 圖面的工具。它提供檢視、量測、批註、目前檢視 PDF 匯出，以及交由 Android 系統列印流程處理等功能。

圖面與本機處理

您透過 Android 系統檔案選擇器（Storage Access Framework，SAF）選取 DWG 檔案。圖面的讀取、解析、顯示、量測和批註均在您的裝置本機進行。MobileCadViewer 不提供帳號系統，也不會將您選取的 DWG、量測結果、批註或文字註記上傳到由開發者營運的伺服器。

PDF 與列印

當您選擇匯出 PDF 時，PDF 在裝置本機產生，並由您選擇 Android 系統提供的儲存位置。當您選擇列印時，MobileCadViewer 會交由 Android System Print Framework 處理；您選擇的印表機、列印服務或儲存目的地可能依其各自的服務與設定處理資料。

資料收集、傳輸與廣告

MobileCadViewer 不包含廣告、分析或遙測 SDK，也不會刻意收集或傳輸您的 DWG 圖面內容、審閱內容或文字註記給開發者。此 App 不需要建立 MobileCadViewer 帳號。

檔案存取與保留

檔案存取由 Android SAF 與您授予的文件選取權限控制，而非要求廣泛的裝置儲存空間存取。DWG 不會由 App 修改或另存。量測與批註是目前開啟檢視中的本機內容；重新開啟圖面或離開目前檢視時，這些暫存內容不會由 MobileCadViewer 上傳或同步。

網路與離線使用

開啟本機 DWG、量測、批註、PDF 匯出與列印準備不依賴 MobileCadViewer 的網路服務。本頁面的連結本身需要瀏覽器連線到網際網路；若裝置離線，外部瀏覽器可能無法載入本頁，但 App 的本機 CAD 工作流程仍可使用。

安全與適用對象

我們採用 Android 的系統文件選取與列印流程，並將圖面處理保留在裝置本機。沒有任何技術措施能保證所有裝置、應用程式或系統服務的絕對安全，請依您的組織要求保護機密圖面。本產品面向一般成人與專業使用者，並非針對兒童設計。

政策更新與聯絡方式

若產品的資料處理方式有實質變更，本政策可能更新，更新後的生效日期將公布於本頁。若對本政策有疑問，請聯絡：mobilecadviewer.support@gmail.com。

MobileCadViewer Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Product and purpose

MobileCadViewer is an offline tool for viewing and reviewing local DWG drawings. It offers viewing, measurement, markup, current-view PDF export, and handoff to Android's system print flow.

Drawings and local processing

You select DWG files through Android's system file picker (Storage Access Framework, or SAF). Reading, parsing, rendering, measurement, and markup occur locally on your device. MobileCadViewer has no account system and does not upload selected DWGs, measurements, markup, or text notes to servers operated by the developer.

PDF and printing

When you export a PDF, it is generated locally on your device and saved to a location you choose through Android's system flow. When you print, MobileCadViewer hands the job to Android System Print Framework. Your chosen printer, print service, or storage destination may handle data according to its own service and settings.

Collection, transmission, and advertising

MobileCadViewer contains no advertising, analytics, or telemetry SDK. It does not intentionally collect or transmit your DWG content, review content, or text notes to the developer. A MobileCadViewer account is not required.

File access and retention

File access is governed by Android SAF and the document permission you grant, rather than broad device-storage access. The app does not modify or save DWGs. Measurements and markup are local to the current open view; when you reopen a drawing or leave the current view, this temporary content is not uploaded or synchronized by MobileCadViewer.

Network and offline use

Opening local DWGs, measurement, markup, PDF export, and print preparation do not depend on a MobileCadViewer network service. The link to this page requires an internet connection in an external browser; if the device is offline, the browser may be unable to load this page while the local CAD workflow remains usable.

Security and audience

We use Android's system document-selection and printing flows and keep drawing processing on-device. No technical measure can guarantee absolute security across every device, app, or system service; protect confidential drawings according to your organization's requirements. The product is intended for general adult and professional users and is not directed to children.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy if the product's data practices materially change. The updated effective date will be posted on this page. For questions, contact mobilecadviewer.support@gmail.com.
